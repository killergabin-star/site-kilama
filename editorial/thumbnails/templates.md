# Editorial thumbnail templates

These templates are reusable composition grammars. They can be used as prompt
templates for image generation, as references for hand-drawn SVGs, or as
layout briefs for Inkscape/Penpot.

## 01. Person spotlight

Use for articles about agency, leadership, decision constraints, teaching, or
individual institutional choices. A single stylized person stands in a visual
field of bars, coins, or policy pressure.

Variations: one figure facing a risk object; figure centered inside red disk;
figure between a rising indicator and a fiscal object.

## 02. Balance bars

Use for distributional trade-offs, public finance, macro imbalance, aid versus
defence, or policy choices. A scale carries bars, institutions, factories, or
social indicators.

Variations: one side institutional, one side industrial; red bar as stress; one
tray overweighted.

## 03. Map arrows

Use for trade, sanctions, geopolitics, energy routes, fragmentation and
transmission channels. A simplified map or route field is crossed by a dominant
red arrow and secondary pale routes.

Variations: one chokepoint; multiple converging routes; blocked route with
bypass arcs.

## 04. Broken chart

Use for scenario breaks, tail risks, polycrisis, financial instability or a
baseline that collapses. A chart or grid is cracked by a red line.

Variations: falling line, broken panel, warning symbol, shock disk.

## 05. Coins cascade

Use for development finance, fiscal compression, aid contraction, debt service,
or household/public-budget squeeze. Coins move along a red curve into a smaller
budget space.

Variations: shrinking coins, public wallet, seedling, narrowing funnel.

## 06. Parliament symbol

Use for institutions, legitimacy, policy reform, public authority, defence
budget decisions, or sovereignty. A parliament-like structure sits under a red
sun/disk.

Variations: scale behind building, road to institution, citizen silhouettes.

## 07. Pipeline valve

Use for energy shocks, Ormuz, oil, gas, critical materials and chokepoints. A
large valve or gauge controls directional pressure.

Variations: red valve, pressure gauge, blocked flow, route arrow.

## 08. Containers bridge

Use for trade infrastructure, logistics, supply chains, corridors, rail, ports,
or physical connectivity. Containers create a bridge or passage under stress.

Variations: missing span, red load-bearing container, water/cliff below.

## 09. Scale vs factories

Use for sanctions, industry, PME, pass-through, production constraints and
industrial policy. A scale or weighing system opposes institutional/legal force
and factories.

Variations: red smoke, factory silhouettes, policy bars, clamp shape.

## 10. Roundtable flows

Use for G7, multilateralism, negotiations, borrower coordination, donor
coordination, and governance forums. A roundtable sits inside circular arrows.

Variations: red agenda disk, asymmetric arrows, empty chair, one blocked flow.
